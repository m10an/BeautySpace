# Current editorial assets

No user-reference photographs were available in the attached files on 2026-11-04. The current interface therefore uses a restrained typographic editorial composition; it does not imitate, generate or copy their portraits. The prior generated serum hero and legacy decorative photographs are no longer rendered by the application. Their original files/provenance remain retained below as historical assets. User-uploaded product/progress photographs are unchanged.

Functional typography: Poppins Regular/SemiBold. Editorial: Cormorant Garamond at Regular 400. Decorative: Pinyon Script Regular, limited to short nonessential heading accents. All three are official Google Fonts families under SIL OFL 1.1; their unmodified source TTFs and per-family licenses are retained. The application requests WOFF2 encodings only (about 288 KB combined); Cormorant is instantiated at weight 400. Exact source URLs and original/derived SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `font-provenance.json`. Fonts are self-hosted and cached offline with system/Georgia/cursive fallbacks. No Google Fonts runtime request is required.
