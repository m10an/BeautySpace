## Safety and preservation baseline

Keep `girly-skincare-v1`, the Hair key, IPL versioned archive, IndexedDB products/photos, all original backup validation and medical/confirmation/duplicate guards. No destructive migrations or automatic calendar entries. Local rule-based consultant remains the default; no billable request/provider is enabled. Do not turn decorative reference product names/ratings or screenshots into real user data. Product photography is only the user's own upload. Historical sample routine is preserved and identified as such rather than overwritten.

## Verification plan

Capture before and after each route and key dialog at desktop and mobile with identical disposable test data/date; include empty shelf and an explicitly marked QA item without invented product photography. Exercise Home/brand/Help, AM/PM completion persistence, product filtering/edit/delete/analysis, consultant interview/errors/edit/reject/confirm/duplicates, IPL preview/confirm/edit/cancel, backup and offline daily quote. Run unchanged checks and unit/E2E/build, plus seven widths in both themes and accessible contrast/keyboard/scroll tests. Any intended default-route changes must be reflected in navigation tests without removing their functional assertions. Real Safari/VoiceOver require an actual supported environment and must not be claimed from Chromium emulation.
